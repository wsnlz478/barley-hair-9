Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about contacting us and booking your hair transplant in China

What is the best way to contact Barley as an international patient
For the quickest response, we recommend reaching out via WhatsApp at +86 13730143529. WhatsApp is our primary channel for overseas patients, and our international coordinators typically respond within 2-4 hours during business hours. WeChat (ID: 15810155700) and email (hairtransplant@qq.com) are also great options.
Do you have staff who speak fluent English
Yes. Our international patient department is staffed entirely by bilingual professionals who speak fluent English and Mandarin. From your first message through your post-op follow-ups, you'll always communicate in English. Many of our surgeons have also trained internationally and can discuss your treatment plan in English during consultations.
How quickly will I hear back after reaching out
We aim to respond to all inquiries within 2-4 hours during our operating hours (9:00 AM to 8:00 PM China Standard Time, seven days a week). Messages sent outside these hours will be answered first thing the next morning. For the fastest turnaround, WhatsApp is your best bet.
How do I book a free hair transplant consultation
It's easy — just send us a message on WhatsApp, WeChat, or email with your name, a brief description of your hair loss, and your preferred consultation date. Our international coordinator will confirm your appointment within 24 hours and send you everything you need, including how to prepare and what to expect.
Can I have a virtual consultation before traveling to China
Absolutely. We actually encourage all international patients to start with an online consultation. During your virtual session, our doctor will assess your hair loss through photos or a video call, discuss treatment options, estimate the number of grafts you'll need, and give you a preliminary cost range. This way, you can make an informed decision before booking any travel.
What should I prepare before my consultation
For the most accurate assessment, please have clear photos of your scalp ready — front, top, back, and both sides. If you have older photos showing your hair from previous years, those are really helpful for understanding your hair loss progression. It's also useful to note any medications you're taking, treatments you've tried, and any medical conditions that might be relevant to surgery.
Is the consultation really free with no strings attached
Yes — 100% free and completely without obligation. Our consultations are designed to help you understand your options, whether you end up choosing Barley or another clinic. We'll never pressure you into booking a procedure. Our goal is simply to give you honest, professional advice so you can make the best decision for yourself.
Do you help with travel arrangements for international patients
We do. Our international patient services include airport pickup, hotel bookings near the clinic, and help with local transportation. Many of our partner hotels offer discounted rates for our patients. We can also provide an official invitation letter if you need one for your visa application. We want your entire trip to go as smoothly as possible.
What does the booking process look like
After your consultation, if you decide to move forward, the booking process is straightforward: (1) We confirm your procedure date, the number of grafts, and the total cost. (2) A deposit is required to secure your spot — we'll send payment instructions based on your preferred method. (3) Once everything's confirmed, we'll send you a detailed prep guide covering what to do in the days before surgery and what to pack for your trip.
How far in advance should I book
We recommend booking at least 4-6 weeks before your preferred travel date, especially during peak seasons (March-May and September-November). This gives you enough time to arrange travel, apply for a visa if needed, and prepare for the procedure. That said, we can sometimes accommodate shorter notice — just get in touch and we'll do our best to help.
What payment methods do you accept from international patients
We accept international bank transfers, credit cards (Visa, MasterCard), and WeChat Pay. For larger procedures, we're happy to discuss payment plan options. The full procedure cost is typically due on the day of surgery. We'll walk you through all pricing and payment details during your consultation so there are no surprises.
Do I need a visa to come to China for a hair transplant
Depending on your nationality, you may need a visa. Most travelers require a tourist visa (L visa) or medical visa to enter China. We can provide an official invitation letter from our medical institution to support your application. We recommend checking the visa requirements for your country and starting the process at least 4 weeks before your planned travel date.
What's the time difference and how does it affect communication
China operates on China Standard Time (CST, UTC+8). That means we're 12-16 hours ahead of the Americas, 7-8 hours ahead of Europe, and 1-2 hours ahead of Southeast Asia. Our business hours are 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM CST. Even if you message us outside these hours, we'll get back to you promptly the next business day. We're very mindful of time differences and do our best to stay responsive.
Can I bring a family member or friend along
Of course — many of our international patients travel with a spouse, partner, or friend. We're happy to accommodate your companion throughout the entire process. They're welcome to join your consultation, be present during pre-op discussions, and stay with you during recovery. We can also help arrange suitable accommodation for both of you.
What kind of aftercare do you provide once I'm back home
After your procedure, you'll receive comprehensive aftercare instructions in English. We schedule follow-up check-ins at 1 week, 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, and 12 months. Our international patient coordinators stay available via WhatsApp and email for any questions during your recovery. We also offer photo-based progress reviews so our surgeons can monitor your growth remotely. You'll never feel alone during your recovery.
What if I need to cancel or reschedule
We know travel plans can change. If you need to reschedule, just let us know at least 7 days before your appointment and we'll gladly find a new date that works for you. Your deposit is fully transferable to your new appointment. If you need to cancel entirely, our cancellation policy will be clearly explained during the booking process — no hidden fees, no surprises.
