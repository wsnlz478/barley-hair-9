Frequently Asked Questions About Our Medical Team

Everything you need to know about our surgeons and their qualifications

What medical licenses and certifications do Barley surgeons hold
Every Barley surgeon holds a practicing medical license issued by China's National Health Commission, and many hold additional board certifications in dermatology or plastic surgery. Our senior physicians also maintain membership in recognized professional bodies such as the Asian Medical Hair Restoration Association and the China Medical Association's Plastic and Aesthetic Surgery Branch.
How many procedures has the average Barley surgeon performed
Our lead surgeons each have personally completed over 5,000 individual hair transplant procedures, with several senior doctors exceeding 10,000 cases. Across the entire medical team, Barley has delivered over 100,000 successful procedures since 2006. This depth of hands-on experience is something very few clinics worldwide can match.
Do you have doctors who can communicate fluently in English
Yes. Multiple members of our clinical team speak fluent English, and our international patient department provides dedicated English-speaking coordinators who accompany you from your first message through your final follow-up. We also offer consultation via WhatsApp and WeChat video calls in English.
Can I request a specific surgeon for my procedure
Absolutely. You can review our full doctor directory and request any surgeon who matches your needs. During your initial consultation, our coordinator will also recommend the physician whose specialization best aligns with your specific case, whether that's hairline design, corrective surgery, or female pattern restoration.
Will the same doctor who consults me also perform the surgery
Yes. The physician who evaluates your scalp, designs your hairline, and plans your graft count will be the same doctor who performs your procedure. This continuity ensures the surgical plan is executed precisely as discussed. The same doctor also handles your immediate post-operative check and follow-up appointments.
Do your doctors specialize in specific types of hair restoration
Yes. While all our surgeons are proficient in the full range of transplant techniques, individual doctors have particular strengths. Some excel at artistic hairline design, others specialize in large-area baldness coverage, corrective surgery for failed transplants, or female diffuse thinning. We match your case to the right specialist.
How does Barley train new surgeons on the microneedle system
Our training program is structured and multi-phase. New surgeons first complete extensive classroom study of our proprietary techniques, then assist on hundreds of cases under senior supervision, then perform procedures with direct oversight before being cleared for independent practice. This apprenticeship model typically takes 12-18 months before a surgeon is fully certified in our system.
Who actually performs the extraction and implantation during surgery
The lead surgeon oversees every step and personally performs the critical phases, including hairline design, recipient site creation, and implantation of the most visible frontal zone grafts. A trained surgical team assists with follicle extraction and graft preparation under the surgeon's direct supervision. This team-based approach maximizes efficiency while maintaining quality control.
Are your doctors experienced with international patients
Our clinical team regularly treats patients from North America, Europe, Southeast Asia, Australia, and the Middle East. Our doctors understand the different hair characteristics, aesthetic expectations, and cultural considerations of various ethnic backgrounds. This international experience ensures your results will look natural within your own ethnic context.
Can I see before-and-after cases performed by a specific doctor
During your consultation, we can show you a portfolio of cases handled by any doctor you're interested in. We maintain detailed case files organized by surgeon, procedure type, and hair loss category. You'll be able to review results from cases similar to your own situation before making your decision.
What happens if I'm not satisfied with my results
Patient satisfaction is taken extremely seriously. If your results don't meet the agreed-upon expectations, the original surgeon will reassess your case and discuss corrective options. In rare situations where graft survival falls below our guaranteed threshold, we offer complimentary touch-up procedures. We document every step of your surgery for full accountability.
Do your doctors also provide non-surgical treatments
Yes. Our physicians are trained to evaluate and recommend the full spectrum of hair loss treatments, including PRP therapy, low-level laser treatment, prescription medications like finasteride and minoxidil, and nutritional supplements. Many patients benefit from a combined approach that pairs surgery with ongoing medical management for optimal long-term density.
How do Barley doctors stay current with global hair restoration trends
Our surgeons regularly attend international conferences including the ISHRS World Congress and regional medical hair restoration symposia. They participate in peer-reviewed research, exchange knowledge with colleagues worldwide, and continuously refine our techniques. Our internal R&D department also develops new tools and protocols based on the latest scientific findings.
What is the doctor-to-patient ratio during a transplant procedure
Each procedure has one lead surgeon, one to two assisting doctors, and a team of trained technicians. For a typical 2,500-graft session, you'll have at least 5-6 medical professionals dedicated to your case. This ensures that extraction, graft preparation, and implantation happen simultaneously and efficiently, keeping your grafts out of the body for the shortest possible time.
Can I schedule a pre-visit video call with my assigned surgeon
Yes. We offer video consultations via WhatsApp, WeChat, or Zoom before your travel. During this call, your assigned surgeon will review photos of your scalp, discuss your goals, explain the recommended approach, and answer any questions. This pre-visit connection helps build confidence before you even board your flight.
Are your doctors involved in post-operative follow-up care
Your surgeon personally examines your scalp before you leave the clinic and schedules follow-up evaluations at 1 week, 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, and 12 months. For international patients, follow-ups are conducted via photo submissions and video calls. The same doctor who performed your surgery reviews your progress at every stage.
