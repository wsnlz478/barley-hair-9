International Patient FAQ

Everything you need to know before your hair transplant journey to China

What visa do I need to get a hair transplant in China
Most international patients enter China on a standard tourist visa (L visa). We provide an official invitation letter that you can submit with your visa application at the nearest Chinese embassy or consulate. Some nationalities are eligible for visa-free transit (up to 144 hours) if you're connecting through certain Chinese cities, which may cover your procedure. Our patient coordinators will guide you through the visa process and recommend the best option based on your nationality and travel plans.
How much can I save by getting a hair transplant in China
Hair transplant costs in China are typically 50-70% lower than in the United States, United Kingdom, Australia, or Western Europe. A procedure that might cost 10,000-15,000 USD in the US often ranges from 2,500-5,000 USD at our clinics. This significant savings includes the procedure itself, post-operative medications, and follow-up care— — delivered with the same or higher quality standards you'd expect at home.
How long should I stay in China for my hair transplant
We recommend planning a 5-7 day trip for your procedure. Day 1 is reserved for an in-person consultation, scalp analysis, and pre-operative testing. Day 2 is typically your procedure day. Days 3-5 allow for initial recovery, post-op check-ups, and gentle cleaning of the transplanted area. If you'd like to explore China or combine your trip with tourism, we can help plan a safe sightseeing itinerary that won't interfere with your healing.
Do you arrange airport pickup and hotel accommodation
Yes, we provide complimentary airport pickup for all international patients. An English-speaking driver will greet you at arrivals with a personalized sign. We've also partnered with several premium hotels within walking distance of our clinics, offering discounted rates exclusively for our patients. Our team can handle your hotel booking, arrange transportation to and from the clinic, and ensure your entire stay is comfortable and stress-free.
Will language be a barrier during my treatment
Not at all. From the moment you contact us, you'll be assigned a dedicated English-speaking patient coordinator who will assist you throughout your entire journey. All consent forms, medical documents, and post-operative care instructions are provided in professional English. Many of our surgeons and nursing staff are fluent in English as well, so you'll never feel misunderstood or left out during consultations, procedures, or follow-ups.
Can I book a free video consultation before traveling
Absolutely. We strongly recommend a free virtual consultation before you commit to travel. During the video call, one of our expert surgeons will review your hair loss pattern, explain your treatment options, estimate the number of grafts you'll need, and provide a transparent preliminary quote. Simply send us clear photos of your scalp from the front, top, and back, along with a brief medical history, and we'll have everything ready for your consultation.
What payment methods do you accept for international patients
We accept Visa, MasterCard, international wire transfers, cash in major currencies (USD, EUR, GBP, etc.), and popular digital payment platforms including Alipay and WeChat Pay. Payment is typically collected on the day of your procedure after you've had a chance to review the final treatment plan. We also offer flexible installment options for larger procedures— —t discuss this with your coordinator during consultation.
Is it safe to fly home after a hair transplant
Yes, flying is perfectly safe after a hair transplant. Most of our international patients board their return flight 3-5 days post-procedure. We provide a travel care kit with all necessary medications, a soft protective cap, and a medical certificate for airport security if needed. On the flight, stay hydrated, avoid pressing your scalp against the headrest, and take brief walks every few hours to promote healthy circulation. Your surgeon will give personalized travel advice based on your specific case.
How do you handle follow-up care once I'm back home
Our international patient program includes comprehensive remote follow-up care. We schedule virtual check-ins at 1 week, 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, and 12 months post-procedure via video call. You'll have direct access to your surgeon through WhatsApp, WeChat, or email for any questions between scheduled visits. We also provide detailed recovery videos, photo-tracking guides, and milestone expectations so you always know exactly what to expect during your healing process.
What if I need a touch-up after my procedure
While our 95%+ graft survival rate makes touch-ups uncommon, we do offer complimentary minor adjustments within the first 12 months if any areas need refinement. For international patients, we first assess the situation through photos and video calls. If a touch-up is warranted, we'll cover the surgical costs— —'d only need to handle your travel expenses. Many patients combine their touch-up visit with a second trip to explore more of China.
Which medications should I stop before my hair transplant
We recommend discontinuing blood thinners (aspirin, ibuprofen, warfarin), certain supplements (fish oil, vitamin E, ginkgo biloba), and any hair loss medications (finasteride, minoxidil) approximately 1-2 weeks before your procedure. However, you should always consult your primary physician before stopping any prescribed medication. We provide a detailed pre-operative checklist during your consultation, including a full list of substances to avoid and a timeline for when to stop each one.
Can I combine my hair transplant with sightseeing in China
Many patients choose to turn their hair transplant trip into a memorable travel experience. After your initial recovery period (approximately 5-7 days), most light activities and sightseeing are perfectly fine. Depending on your clinic location, you can visit iconic attractions like the Great Wall, the Forbidden City, the Bund in Shanghai, or enjoy the vibrant cuisine of Guangzhou. We'll advise you on safe activities and sun protection to keep your healing scalp in optimal condition while you explore.
Are there any hidden fees I should be aware of
We believe in complete transparency with our pricing. The quote you receive during consultation covers the full procedure, including anesthesia, surgical fees, post-operative medications, and all scheduled follow-up visits. Airport pickup and virtual consultations are complimentary. The only additional costs you might incur are personal expenses such as meals, hotel upgrades beyond our recommended options, or optional tourism activities. There are absolutely no surprise charges on procedure day.
Is a hair transplant in China as safe as in my home country
Our clinics meet or exceed international safety standards. All procedures are performed in certified operating rooms with full sterility protocols, board-certified surgeons, and comprehensive emergency equipment. We hold 8 Sullivan certifications— — global benchmark for medical quality— —閿 14 proprietary patents for our microneedle technology. Our graft survival rate of over 95% is among the highest in the world, reflecting both our technical expertise and our uncompromising commitment to patient safety.
What is the best time of year to travel to China for a hair transplant
You can schedule your hair transplant in China year-round— — indoor climate-controlled clinics ensure consistent conditions regardless of the season. That said, spring (March-May) and autumn (September-November) offer the most comfortable weather for combining your procedure with sightseeing. Summer months can be hot and humid in many Chinese cities, while winters in northern cities like Beijing can be quite cold. We recommend planning around your personal schedule and preferred climate for any post-recovery travel.
