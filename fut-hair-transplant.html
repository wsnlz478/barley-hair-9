Understanding the FUT Technique

Follicular Unit Transplantation, commonly known as the strip method, is one of the most time-tested approaches to permanent hair restoration. During the procedure, a skilled surgeon removes a narrow strip of tissue from the back of your scalp — the area that is naturally most resistant to male pattern baldness. This tissue is then carefully divided into individual follicular units under high-powered microscopes, and each graft is precisely transplanted into the thinning or balding areas of your scalp.

While FUE has gained popularity in recent years, the strip technique remains the gold standard for patients who need the maximum number of grafts in a single session. At Barley Hospital, we have refined the traditional FUT approach with modern trichophytic closure techniques. This advanced method allows hair to grow directly through the scar line, making it virtually unnoticeable compared to older strip methods used by many other clinics worldwide.

FUT vs FUE: Choosing the Right Method

Understanding the key differences to make an informed decision

FUT (Strip Method)

  • 3,000-5,000+ grafts per session
  • Lower cost per graft
  • Single linear scar (hidden by hair)
  • 4-6 hour procedure
  • Desk work: 3-5 days recovery
  • Best for advanced hair loss

FUE (Individual Extraction)

  • 2,500-4,000 grafts per session
  • Higher cost per graft
  • Tiny dot scars (virtually invisible)
  • 6-8 hour procedure
  • Desk work: 1-3 days recovery
  • Ideal for short hairstyles

When FUT Is the Best Option for You

Three key scenarios where FUT delivers superior results

Advanced Hair Loss

If you are at Norwood stage 6 or 7 and need over 3,500 grafts in one session, FUT provides the coverage you need when other techniques simply cannot deliver enough grafts.

Higher Total Graft Count

Across multiple sessions, FUT can often provide more total grafts than FUE due to the way it preserves the donor area, making it the best choice for full-scalp restoration cases.

More Affordable Option

With a lower cost per graft than FUE, FUT delivers extensive coverage at a significantly reduced price — especially when you choose to have your procedure performed at our clinics in China.

Frequently Asked Questions About FUT

Answers to the questions patients ask us most often

What exactly is an FUT hair transplant
FUT (Follicular Unit Transplantation) is a permanent hair restoration technique where a thin strip of scalp tissue is surgically removed from the donor area, typically the back of the head. The strip is then divided into individual follicular units under high-powered microscopes and transplanted into thinning or balding areas. It is the most established and extensively studied hair transplant method, with decades of proven clinical results.
Does FUT leave a visible scar
FUT leaves a single linear scar at the donor site. However, with modern trichophytic closure techniques, hair actually grows through the scar line, making it virtually invisible as long as you keep your hair at least 1-2 cm long at the back. At Barley, our surgeons use advanced multi-layer suturing methods that significantly reduce scar visibility compared to older FUT techniques used by many other clinics worldwide.
Who is the ideal candidate for FUT
FUT is best suited for patients with extensive hair loss (Norwood 6-7) who need the maximum number of grafts in a single session. It is also an excellent choice for people who plan to wear their hair long enough to cover the donor area, patients seeking the most cost-effective option for large-scale restoration, and those with good scalp elasticity. If maximum coverage in one session is your top priority, FUT is often the right answer.
FUT vs FUE: which method is better for me
FUT can harvest significantly more grafts (3,000-5,000+) in a single session and costs less per graft, but it does leave a linear scar and requires a slightly longer recovery period. FUE leaves no linear scarring and heals faster, but yields fewer grafts per session and costs more per graft. If you need maximum coverage and plan to keep your hair long enough to conceal the scar, FUT may be the better option. For patients who prefer very short hairstyles or want the fastest recovery, FUE is the preferred choice.
How many grafts can be harvested in one FUT session
A single FUT session typically harvests between 3,000 and 5,000 grafts, and in some cases even more depending on your donor area capacity. This makes FUT the preferred method for patients with advanced hair loss who need extensive coverage in a single procedure. The exact number depends on your scalp elasticity, donor hair density, and the size of the area that needs treatment.
How painful is the FUT procedure
FUT is performed under local anaesthesia, so you will not feel any pain during the procedure itself. Most patients describe the experience as comfortable — many even listen to music or take a nap during surgery. After the anaesthesia wears off, some mild discomfort is normal for the first two to three days, which is easily managed with prescribed pain medication. Most patients report that the sensation feels more like mild tightness rather than actual pain.
What is trichophytic closure and why is it so important
Trichophytic closure is an advanced surgical suturing technique where the wound edges are carefully overlapped at a specific angle so that hair follicles can grow directly through the resulting scar line. This is fundamentally different from traditional FUT closure methods where the scar remains completely bare. The result is a scar that blends naturally with surrounding hair, making it virtually undetectable. It is widely regarded as one of the most significant advancements in FUT surgery.
How long is the recovery period after FUT
Most patients can return to desk-based work within 3-5 days after FUT surgery. Full healing of the donor area takes approximately 2 weeks. Stitches or staples are typically removed 10-14 days after the procedure. The scar will gradually fade, flatten, and soften over the following 3-6 months. While you should avoid strenuous exercise for at least 2 weeks, most normal daily activities can be resumed within the first week.
Can I choose FUT if I wear my hair very short
If you regularly wear your hair very short (such as a buzz cut or close crop), FUT may not be the best choice for you because the linear scar could be visible at very short hair lengths. In such cases, FUE is a far better option since it leaves only tiny, scattered dot scars that are virtually undetectable even on closely shaved heads. During your consultation, we will assess your preferred hairstyle and recommend accordingly.
Is FUT significantly cheaper than FUE
Yes, FUT is generally more affordable on a per-graft basis compared to FUE. Because FUT can harvest a larger number of grafts in a single session, the overall cost for large-scale procedures is often substantially lower. In China, FUT prices at Barley Hospital can be 50-70% lower than equivalent procedures in the United States, United Kingdom, or Western Europe. For patients needing extensive coverage, this cost difference can amount to thousands of pounds or dollars in savings.
What happens to the strip after it is removed from my scalp
After the strip is carefully removed, it is immediately handed to our skilled technical team. Using high-powered stereo microscopes, they meticulously separate the strip into individual follicular units, each containing one to four hairs. These grafts are then sorted by size and quality, kept in a special nutrient solution to maintain viability, and prepared for precise implantation into the recipient area. The entire process is carried out with the utmost care to maximise graft survival rates.
Can FUT and FUE be combined in a treatment plan
Yes, many patients benefit from a combined approach. FUT can be used in the first session to harvest a large number of grafts for the primary restoration, and FUE can be used later to add density in specific areas or for a follow-up procedure. This hybrid strategy maximises the total number of available grafts and can be particularly useful for patients with very advanced hair loss. Our surgeons will design the optimal plan for your individual case.
When will I see the final results after FUT
The transplanted hair will typically shed within 2-4 weeks after surgery, which is a completely normal part of the process. New hair growth usually begins at around 3-4 months. You will notice a clear improvement in density by 6-9 months. Full, mature results are visible at 12-18 months post-surgery, when the transplanted hair has reached its final thickness and natural appearance. We provide regular follow-up assessments throughout this entire journey.
Does FUT thin out the donor area permanently
The donor area may appear very slightly thinner after FUT since a narrow strip of tissue is removed. However, because the strip is taken from the densest part of the donor zone, the visual impact is typically minimal and often unnoticeable. Your surgeon carefully calculates the strip width to ensure that the remaining donor area retains sufficient density. The hair surrounding the scar line grows completely normally.
Why should I consider travelling to China for my FUT procedure
China offers world-class FUT surgery at a fraction of the cost charged by Western clinics. Barley Hospital brings over 18 years of experience, a network of 30+ clinics across major Chinese cities, and more than 100,000 successful procedures to date. International patients can save 50-70% compared to equivalent procedures in the US, UK, or Europe, while receiving treatment from highly experienced surgeons who use the latest techniques and technology. We also provide full English-language support from your first consultation through to your final follow-up.
Can I have a second FUT procedure if I have already had one before
In many cases, yes. If you have sufficient remaining donor area tissue and good scalp elasticity, a second FUT procedure can be performed. Our surgeons will carefully evaluate your donor area during the consultation to determine whether a repeat FUT is feasible. If donor area limitations make a second FUT unsuitable, FUE may be recommended as an alternative for any additional grafts you may need.
How should I prepare before my FUT surgery
In the week before surgery, avoid blood-thinning medications and supplements such as aspirin, vitamin E, and fish oil. You should also avoid alcohol and stop smoking at least 48 hours before your procedure, as smoking can negatively affect healing. Wash your hair thoroughly on the morning of surgery and wear a button-up shirt so you do not need to pull anything over your head afterwards. Arrange for someone to drive you home, as you may be given mild sedatives during the procedure.
