About Our Clinic

Everything you need to know about Barley Hair Transplant Hospital

What makes Barley Hair Transplant Hospital different from other clinics in China
Barley is China's original microneedle hair transplant pioneer. Since 2006, we've built our reputation on proprietary technology— —cifically our 360rotating implant pen that creates incisions as small as 0.6mm, delivering up to 30% higher graft density than conventional methods. We operate 30+ directly managed clinics (not franchises), maintain 14 patents, and have successfully treated over 100,000 patients from 50+ countries. This scale, combined with our Sullivan-certified quality standards, sets us apart from nearly every other hair restoration provider in China.
Who founded Barley Hair Transplant Hospital and what was the vision
Barley was founded in 2006 by a group of hair restoration surgeons and biomedical engineers who believed that hair transplantation could be significantly more precise, less invasive, and produce more natural-looking results. Their vision was to develop a technology that minimized scalp trauma while maximizing graft survival— —閿 that led to the creation of our proprietary microneedle implant pen. Nearly two decades later, that original vision continues to drive everything we do.
What core values guide Barley's approach to patient care
Our six core values are: Patient-Centered Care (your goals drive every decision), Continuous Innovation (we never stop improving), Outstanding Quality (no shortcuts, ever), Full Transparency (honest pricing and realistic expectations), Collaborative Teamwork (surgeons, technicians, and coordinators work as one unit), and International Service (we make overseas patients feel completely at home). These aren't just words on a wall— —y're embedded in how we train staff, design procedures, and follow up with every patient.
How many patents does Barley hold and what do they cover
We currently hold 14 proprietary patents covering every stage of the hair transplant process: the 360rotating microneedle implant pen, precision follicle extraction tools, graft storage optimization methods, angle-specific implantation techniques, and advanced no-shave extraction protocols. These patents are the result of nearly 20 years of clinical research and represent significant competitive advantages over clinics using standard or outdated equipment.
What is the Sullivan certification and how many has Barley earned
The Sullivan certification is a globally recognized medical quality standard that evaluates clinical outcomes, patient safety protocols, facility standards, and operational excellence. Barley has earned 8 Sullivan certifications across multiple areas including microneedle hair transplantation excellence, surgical safety standards, and international patient care. This certification is independently audited and places us among the top-tier hair restoration providers worldwide.
Does Barley have clinic locations outside of China
All 30+ of our directly managed clinics are currently located within China, primarily in tier-1 and tier-2 cities including Shanghai, Beijing, Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Chengdu, Hangzhou, Nanjing, and many others. While we don't have physical locations abroad, our international patient program is specifically designed to make traveling to China seamless and comfortable for overseas patients. We provide visa support, English-speaking coordinators, airport pickup, and hotel assistance for every international visitor.
How do you ensure identical quality across all 30+ clinic locations
We operate a centralized quality management system. All surgeons complete the same intensive training program at our flagship facility, every clinic uses identical patented equipment sourced from our central supply, and we conduct unannounced quality audits at each location quarterly. Additionally, patient outcomes are tracked across all clinics and reviewed by our medical board. This standardized approach means that whether you're treated in Shanghai or Xi'an, the technology, protocols, and quality benchmarks are exactly the same.
What exactly is your microneedle technology and why is it superior
Our microneedle implant pen uses ultra-fine needles (0.6-1.0mm diameter) to create recipient sites and implant follicles simultaneously, eliminating the need for pre-made incisions. This reduces scalp trauma by approximately one-third compared to traditional FUE forceps implantation. The 360rotating barrel allows surgeons to match each follicle's natural growth angle and direction precisely, resulting in higher density and completely undetectable results. Patients also benefit from faster healing— —t can resume normal hair washing within 24 hours.
How many successful procedures has Barley completed
Since our founding in 2006, we've successfully performed over 100,000 hair transplant procedures. Our patient satisfaction rate exceeds 98%, and our average graft survival rate is over 95%. We've treated patients from more than 50 countries, meaning our surgical teams have experience with every hair type, texture, and pattern of hair loss— —m Asian straight hair to African textured hair to Caucasian wavy hair. This volume and diversity of experience is unmatched by most clinics.
Are Barley's surgeons recognized in the international medical community
Yes. Our medical team has been invited multiple times to present at the ISHRS (International Society of Hair Restoration Surgery) World Congress— — most prestigious global gathering of hair transplant professionals. Our surgeons have published peer-reviewed papers on microneedle transplantation outcomes, and several hold positions on international hair restoration education boards. This level of global recognition reflects the quality and innovation that Barley brings to the field.
What is your graft survival rate compared to industry averages
Our microneedle technology combined with highly experienced surgical teams produces a graft survival rate exceeding 95%. The global industry average typically ranges from 85-92%. Our superior rate is achieved through meticulous graft handling (follicles are kept in temperature-controlled storage throughout the procedure), minimal extraction trauma, precise recipient site creation, and rapid implantation within the optimal viability window. We stand behind our results and discuss realistic survival expectations during every consultation.
Can I view verified before-and-after photos of Barley patients
Absolutely. Our Cases page features a comprehensive gallery of authentic before-and-after photos organized by treatment type (hairline restoration, baldness correction, eyebrow transplant, beard transplant, and more). All photos are taken under consistent lighting conditions without digital enhancement. During your consultation, we can also show you cases specifically matched to your hair loss pattern, hair type, and desired outcome so you have the clearest possible picture of what to expect.
What kind of training do Barley surgeons go through
Every Barley surgeon begins as a board-certified plastic surgeon or dermatologist with at least 5 years of clinical experience. They then complete an additional 12-18 months of specialized hair transplant training at our flagship facility, where they observe and assist on hundreds of procedures before performing their first independent surgery. Ongoing education is mandatory— — surgeons attend quarterly peer reviews and annual international conferences to stay current with the latest techniques and research.
How can I independently verify Barley's certifications and credentials
All 14 of our patents are publicly searchable through the China National Intellectual Property Administration (CNIPA) database. Our 8 Sullivan certifications can be independently verified through the Sullivan organization's official website. We're also happy to provide copies of all credentials, clinic licenses, and surgeon board certifications during your consultation or upon request. We believe that verifiable transparency builds trust, and we encourage patients to do their own due diligence.
Does Barley offer English-language services for international patients
Yes, comprehensive English support is a cornerstone of our international patient program. From your first inquiry through your final follow-up, you'll work with a dedicated English-speaking patient coordinator. Consent forms, medical records, pre-operative instructions, and aftercare guides are all provided in professional English. Many of our surgeons are fluent in English and can conduct consultations and post-op discussions directly in English. We also offer WhatsApp, WeChat, and email support in English 7 days a week.
