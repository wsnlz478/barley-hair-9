Scar Treatment

Scar hair transplants are commonly misunderstood as simple lines or marks on the skin. They are actually areas of damaged tissue that remain after the skin heals from an injury. Scars can be raised or flat, smooth or bumpy, pinkish-brown or have a depression in the center where it was torn open.

Scars are caused by the body's natural healing process and can occur after an injury or as a result of surgery. When a scar appears on the scalp or face, it has a negative impact on one's mood and social interactions. If you have a visible scar in your brow, beard, or any other area of your head, scar treatment can help you cover visible scars and restore hair growth in the affected area.

Procedure

A hair transplant is a cosmetic procedure in which healthy hair is transplanted from a 'donor' area, usually the back of the head, to a 'recipient' area where it is needed. When someone gets a scar in an area that requires hair to cover, it's important to let the doctor examine them personally to see if there are any blood circulation issues that could lead to more problems later on.

Following a thorough examination and consultation, our experts recommend a suitable course of action. These surgeries, which produce quick and permanent results, are performed using a variety of surgical options such as FUE, FUT, and Barley's Microneedle method.

Scar treatment employs cutting-edge technology, including the use of an Implanter Pen to implant grafted hair follicles. Implanter pens are ideal for implanting grafts, and Barley experts can smoothly control the density and direction of the implanted follicle.

A hair transplant may be an effective treatment for scars on the scalp, brows, and beard. While long-term treatments such as laser therapy or cryotherapy cannot reduce scars, hair transplant is an excellent alternative that may also help improve the appearance of other aspects of your face.

Frequently Asked Questions About Scar Hair Transplant

Honest answers to the questions patients ask most

Can hair be transplanted into a scar
Yes, hair can be transplanted into scar tissue, though the procedure requires special expertise. Scar tissue has different blood supply and texture compared to normal skin, so the surgeon must carefully evaluate the scar's maturity, thickness, and blood circulation before proceeding. At Barley, our experienced surgeons use specialized techniques with our patented microneedle technology to ensure optimal graft survival in scarred areas.
How long should I wait after getting a scar before considering a hair transplant
We generally recommend waiting at least 12 months after the injury or surgery to allow the scar to fully mature and stabilize. This ensures the tissue has adequate blood supply to support transplanted hair follicles. During your consultation, our surgeons will assess the scar's readiness and advise you on the optimal timing for the best results.
What types of scars can be treated with hair transplantation
We treat various types of scars including FUT strip scars, burn scars, injury scars, surgical scars, and acne scars. Each type requires a different approach — FUT scars tend to have predictable results, while burn scars may require multiple sessions due to compromised blood supply. Our surgeons will evaluate your specific scar type and recommend the best treatment plan during your consultation.
What is the success rate of hair transplants in scar tissue
While graft survival rates in scar tissue are slightly lower than in normal skin (typically 85-92% compared to our standard 98%), our microneedle technology significantly improves outcomes. The precision of our 0.6mm extraction tools minimizes trauma to the delicate scar tissue, and our specialized implantation technique ensures optimal follicle placement. Most patients achieve excellent coverage that effectively camouflages the scar.
Will the scar be completely invisible after the transplant
In most cases, the scar becomes significantly less visible after hair grows through the transplanted area. The hair effectively camouflages the scar, making it nearly undetectable in normal social interactions. However, complete invisibility depends on factors such as scar size, type, and the contrast between your hair color and skin tone. Our surgeons will provide realistic expectations during your consultation.
Does scar hair transplant require special aftercare
Scar transplants require the same standard aftercare as regular hair transplants, with a few additional precautions. You'll need to keep the area clean, avoid touching or scratching the treated area, and follow our detailed post-operative instructions. Because scar tissue has different healing characteristics, we may recommend additional follow-up visits to monitor progress and ensure optimal graft survival.
How many sessions are needed for scar coverage
Most small to medium scars can be covered in a single session. Larger or more complex scars, particularly burn scars with compromised blood supply, may require two sessions to achieve optimal density. During your consultation, our surgeons will assess the scar's size and condition to determine the most efficient treatment plan for your specific situation.
What makes Barley's approach to scar transplants different
Barley uses proprietary 0.6mm microneedle technology that creates significantly smaller incisions than conventional tools. This is especially important for scar tissue, which has limited blood supply and healing capacity. Our patented 360— rotation implant pen ensures precise angle and direction control, while our senior surgeons' extensive experience with scar cases ensures the best possible outcomes. We've successfully treated thousands of scar patients from over 50 countries.
