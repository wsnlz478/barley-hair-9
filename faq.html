Your Hair Transplant Questions, Answered

Honest, straightforward answers from our surgical team to help you make an informed decision

What exactly is a hair transplant and how does it work
A hair transplant relocates healthy hair follicles from the permanent donor zone at the back of your head to thinning or bald areas. Because these donor follicles carry a genetic resistance to the hormone DHT that causes pattern baldness, they continue growing in their new location for the rest of your life. Modern techniques use individual follicular unit extraction with microneedle precision for natural-looking results.
What causes male and female pattern hair loss
The leading cause is androgenetic alopecia, driven by a combination of genetics and the hormone dihydrotestosterone (DHT). DHT progressively shrinks susceptible hair follicles until they stop producing visible hair. Other contributing factors include chronic stress, hormonal imbalances, certain medications, nutritional deficiencies, autoimmune conditions, and natural aging. A proper diagnosis during consultation helps determine the best treatment approach.
Am I a good candidate for a hair transplant
Good candidates typically have stable hair loss, adequate donor hair at the back and sides of the scalp, and realistic expectations. Most surgeons recommend waiting until at least age 25, as hair loss patterns in the early twenties are often still evolving. There is no strict upper age limit; patients in their 60s and 70s undergo successful procedures provided they are in reasonable health. Your surgeon will assess your candidacy during a free consultation.
How many grafts will I need for my procedure
Graft count depends on your level of hair loss and the area needing coverage. A receding hairline (Norwood 2-3) typically requires 1,500-2,500 grafts. Moderate thinning (Norwood 4) usually needs 2,500-3,500 grafts. Advanced baldness (Norwood 5-7) can require 4,000-6,000 grafts, often split across two sessions. Your surgeon provides an accurate estimate after examining your donor supply and mapping your scalp.
Will a hair transplant stop me from losing more hair
The transplanted hair itself is permanent and will not fall out. However, your existing native hair may continue thinning over time. Many surgeons recommend complementary treatments such as finasteride or minoxidil to stabilize ongoing loss in untreated areas. Your doctor will discuss a long-term hair maintenance plan tailored to your needs.
Are the results of a hair transplant permanent
Yes. The follicles harvested from the permanent donor zone retain their genetic resistance to DHT after transplantation. This means the transplanted hair will keep growing indefinitely. You may experience initial shedding in the first few weeks after surgery, but these are the hair shafts, not the follicles. New growth begins around month 3 and continues for life.
Is a hair transplant safe What are the risks
Hair transplantation is one of the safest cosmetic procedures when performed by qualified surgeons. Complication rates are very low. Minor side effects may include temporary swelling, redness, mild discomfort, and numbness in the treated areas, all of which resolve within days to weeks. Serious complications such as infection are rare at accredited clinics that follow strict sterile protocols. Choosing a reputable clinic with experienced surgeons is the single most important factor in ensuring safety.
Can I get a hair transplant if I have diffuse thinning rather than a receding hairline
Yes. Diffuse thinning is common in both men and women. Our precision microneedle technique allows individual grafts to be interspersed among existing thinning hairs without damaging native follicles. The key is having enough stable donor hair to work with. During your consultation, your surgeon will evaluate your specific pattern and recommend the most effective approach.
What is the difference between FUE and FUT
FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction) removes individual follicles using tiny punches, leaving only microdot scars that become virtually invisible. FUT (Follicular Unit Transplantation) removes a strip of scalp tissue from the donor area, creating a linear scar. FUE is our primary method because it is less invasive, heals faster, and allows shorter hairstyles. FUT is occasionally considered for very large graft sessions when donor characteristics make it advantageous.
What is DHI (Direct Hair Implantation) and is it better than FUE
Standard FUE involves creating recipient sites first, then placing grafts with forceps. DHI uses an implant pen that simultaneously creates the channel and deposits the graft in one motion. This reduces graft handling time outside the body and allows for higher planting density. Our microneedle system is an advanced form of DHI featuring a patented 360-degree rotating mechanism that gives our surgeons superior angular control for natural hair direction.
Is the procedure painful
The entire procedure is performed under local anesthesia. The only noticeable sensation is a brief pinching or stinging from the initial anesthetic injections, lasting roughly 10-15 seconds. Once the scalp is numb, you feel gentle pressure but no pain. During the 6-8 hour surgery, you can watch movies, listen to music, eat, read, or even nap. Post-operative discomfort is typically mild and easily managed with prescribed pain medication.
How long does the surgery take
A standard session of 2,000-3,500 grafts typically requires 6-8 hours. Larger sessions exceeding 4,000 grafts may be split across two consecutive days to maintain optimal graft quality and patient comfort. The surgical team works efficiently in parallel with extraction, preparation, and implantation happening simultaneously to keep grafts out of the body for the shortest possible time.
What is a no-shave hair transplant
In a standard FUE procedure, the donor area is trimmed short so the surgeon has clear visibility during extraction. A no-shave approach extracts individual follicles from between your existing longer hairs while leaving the rest of your hair completely untouched. The result is total discretion; nobody can tell you have had surgery. The procedure takes slightly longer due to the added precision required to work around existing hair.
Can the same technique be used for beard and eyebrow restoration
Yes. Facial hair transplants follow the same follicular unit extraction principles but require specialized needle configurations and surgical planning. Beard transplants fill patchy areas or create fuller coverage using scalp follicles that adapt to facial growth patterns. Eyebrow transplants demand meticulous angle control to replicate the natural feather-like brow direction, making our 360-degree rotation system particularly valuable for achieving realistic results.
Who performs the surgery - the doctor or technicians
At Barley, qualified, board-certified surgeons personally perform every critical step of the procedure, including donor extraction, recipient site creation, and graft implantation. This differs from many clinics where technicians handle portions of the surgery. Our surgeons have decades of combined experience and oversee the entire process from consultation to final implantation. You can ask about your specific surgeon's credentials during your consultation.
Do I need to stop any medications before surgery
You will receive detailed pre-operative instructions during your consultation. Generally, blood-thinning medications such as aspirin, ibuprofen, and certain supplements (e.g., vitamin E, fish oil, ginkgo) should be paused 7-10 days before surgery to minimize bleeding risk. Prescription medications should never be stopped without consulting your primary care physician. If you take finasteride or minoxidil, your surgeon will advise whether to continue or pause them.
When can I wash my hair after the transplant
With our microneedle technique, you can gently wash your scalp within 24 hours. We provide a specialized mild shampoo and step-by-step washing instructions. The first wash should be a gentle pour-and-pat method, avoiding direct water pressure on the transplanted area. By day 5, normal gentle shampooing is fine. The micro-incisions close rapidly, unlike traditional methods that require 5-7 days of protection.
How soon can I return to work after surgery
Patients with desk-based jobs can typically return within 2-3 days, often wearing a loose-fitting hat to conceal any visible redness. Those with physically demanding roles should plan for 7-10 days off. We provide a medical certificate for your employer if needed. Many international patients feel comfortable flying home within 5-7 days after the procedure.
What is shock loss and should I be worried
Shock loss is the temporary shedding of transplanted hairs that typically occurs 2-6 weeks after surgery. Some existing native hairs near the recipient area may also shed temporarily. This is a completely normal and expected part of the healing process. The follicles remain healthy beneath the skin and will begin producing new visible hair within 3-4 months. Understanding this in advance helps many patients avoid unnecessary worry.
When will I see visible results
Hair growth follows a predictable timeline. Months 1-2: the shedding phase as transplanted hairs fall out. Month 3: the first fine new hairs begin to emerge. Months 4-6: you will notice real thickening and improved coverage. Months 7-9: substantial density development becomes visible. Months 12-18: full maturation with maximum shaft thickness and final density. Patience is essential - the best results reveal themselves gradually.
What activities should I avoid during recovery
For the first 14 days, avoid strenuous exercise, heavy lifting, swimming, saunas, steam rooms, and prolonged direct sun exposure to the scalp. Light walking is encouraged from day one to promote circulation. After two weeks, you can gradually resume normal workouts. Contact sports and activities requiring helmets should be avoided for at least one month to protect the healing grafts.
Will there be visible scarring
FUE extraction leaves tiny dot scars approximately 0.8-1.0mm in diameter that fade to near invisibility within a few months. The recipient area micro-channels (0.6-1.0mm) heal without noticeable marks. If you keep your hair extremely short (a buzz cut or skin fade), trained eyes may detect subtle textural differences. For normal hair lengths, no scarring is visible to the naked eye.
What about swelling after surgery
Mild to moderate swelling of the forehead and around the eyes is common on days 2-4 after surgery. It is a normal inflammatory response and resolves on its own. Sleeping with your head elevated for the first few nights, applying cold compresses to the forehead (never directly on grafts), and staying well-hydrated can significantly reduce swelling. Your surgeon will provide specific anti-swelling instructions.
Can I drink alcohol or smoke after the procedure
We strongly advise avoiding alcohol for at least 5-7 days before and after surgery, as it can increase bleeding and slow healing. Smoking is one of the biggest risks to graft survival - nicotine constricts blood vessels and reduces oxygen delivery to newly implanted follicles. We recommend quitting at least two weeks before and after the procedure for optimal results.
What is the actual graft survival rate at your clinic
Clinical data from our procedures consistently shows graft survival rates exceeding 98%. This industry-leading figure is achieved through our proprietary microneedle implant pen, cold-chain graft storage at 4-8 degrees Celsius, minimal ex-vivo handling time, and meticulous surgical protocols. We document and guarantee our results for full accountability.
Will the transplanted hair look natural or obvious
The results are completely natural for several reasons: the follicles are your own living hair, they retain their original color and texture, and our 360-degree rotation system places each graft at the precise angle matching your natural growth pattern. Single-hair grafts are strategically placed along the frontal hairline for soft, undetectable transitions. Even at close range, the results are indistinguishable from natural growth.
How is the hairline designed for each patient
Our surgeons combine AI-assisted facial proportion analysis with years of artistic experience. The software maps your facial symmetry, forehead height, age-appropriate hairline position, and ethnic characteristics. The surgeon then refines this digital design based on professional aesthetic judgment, ensuring the hairline complements your unique facial structure rather than following a generic template.
Can women achieve results comparable to men
Yes. While female hair loss typically presents as diffuse thinning across the top of the scalp rather than a receding hairline, our precision microneedle technique is exceptionally well-suited for this pattern. Individual grafts are carefully interspersed among existing thinning hairs without damaging native follicles. Female patients also benefit from eyebrow transplants and hairline lowering procedures.
What happens if my results don't meet expectations
Patient satisfaction is a cornerstone of our practice. If your graft survival falls below the guaranteed threshold, we offer a complimentary corrective procedure. Before surgery, your surgeon sets realistic expectations based on your donor supply, hair characteristics, and the extent of your hair loss. We maintain detailed surgical records to ensure full transparency and accountability at every stage.
How can I track my own progress at home
We provide every patient with a structured photo guide specifying the exact angles, distances, and lighting conditions to use. Take photos monthly from consistent positions and compare them side-by-side. These photos are also submitted to your surgeon during scheduled remote follow-ups. Many patients are pleasantly surprised by the month-by-month improvement visible in their own documentation.
Will transplanted hair grow long enough to style or cut
Absolutely. Transplanted hair grows at the same rate as the hair in the donor area. Once fully matured (around 12-18 months), you can cut, style, dye, and treat it exactly like your natural hair. The only difference is that these hairs are genetically resistant to DHT, so they will continue growing for life without falling out due to pattern baldness.
Can I get a second hair transplant if I need more density later
Yes, many patients undergo a second procedure to add density or address areas of continued thinning. A second session is typically planned 12-18 months after the first to allow full maturation of initial results. Your surgeon will assess your remaining donor supply during consultation to determine if a second session is feasible and how many additional grafts are available.
How much does a hair transplant cost in China
Prices at premium clinics like Barley typically range from 2,500 to 5,000 USD depending on graft count and technique selected. This represents savings of 50-70% compared to equivalent procedures in the United States, United Kingdom, or Australia, where the same treatment often costs 8,000-15,000. Despite the lower price, the technology, surgeon qualifications, and clinical standards meet or exceed those of top Western clinics.
What determines the final price of my procedure
The primary cost driver is the number of grafts required. Additional factors include the specific technique chosen (standard FUE, no-shave, or premium microneedle DHI), whether you need a specialized procedure such as eyebrow or beard transplant, and any complementary treatments like PRP therapy. Your personalized quote is provided during the free consultation so you know exactly what to expect.
What payment methods do you accept
We accept international credit cards (Visa, Mastercard), bank wire transfers, Alipay, WeChat Pay, and cash in major currencies including CNY, USD, and EUR. A small deposit secures your appointment date, with the remaining balance due on the day of your procedure. We provide official receipts and detailed invoices for your records.
Does health insurance cover hair transplant surgery
Most insurance providers classify hair transplants as elective cosmetic procedures and do not provide coverage. In rare cases where hair loss results from documented trauma, burns, or a medical condition, partial coverage may be available. We provide comprehensive medical documentation and itemized invoices that may support reimbursement claims. We always recommend checking with your insurer before the procedure.
Are there any hidden fees or surprise charges
The quoted price covers the complete procedure, all surgical materials, post-operative medications, specialized shampoo, and all scheduled follow-up consultations. Travel, accommodation, and meals are your own responsibility, though we provide complimentary airport pickup and negotiated hotel rates through our partner properties. What we quote is what you pay - no surprise charges are ever added.
Is a hair transplant in China cheaper than in Turkey
Pricing is broadly comparable between China and Turkey. The key differentiator is technology level and surgeon involvement. Many Turkish clinics employ technicians rather than board-certified surgeons for the critical implantation phase. At Barley, qualified surgeons personally lead every procedure using advanced 0.6mm microneedle technology versus the 0.8-1.0mm instruments commonly used elsewhere. You receive superior precision at a competitive price.
Do you offer financing or installment plans
While we do not offer in-house financing for international patients, many of our patients use medical travel financing providers in their home countries. We accept payment in installments for local patients through partnered financial institutions. Our coordinator can discuss the available options with you during consultation and help you find a payment structure that works for your budget.
What is included in my treatment package
Your treatment package includes: a comprehensive pre-operative consultation, the full surgical procedure, all surgical supplies and medications, post-operative care kit with specialized shampoo, a medical certificate, remote follow-up consultations for 12 months, and lifetime access to our patient support team. Accommodation and travel are not included but can be arranged at preferential rates through our partner hotels.
What visa do I need to travel to China for a hair transplant
Most patients use a standard tourist (L) visa. Citizens of certain countries may qualify for 72-hour or 144-hour visa-free transit through designated Chinese airports, which is often sufficient for a hair transplant trip. For medical-specific entry, a short-term private affairs (S2) visa is also available. We provide an official invitation letter from the clinic to support your visa application regardless of the type you choose. Visa requirements vary by nationality, so we recommend checking with your nearest Chinese embassy or consulate.
How many days should I plan for my trip to Beijing
We recommend a minimum 5-7 day itinerary. Day 1: arrival and in-person consultation. Day 2: the transplant surgery. Days 3-5: recovery in Beijing with a follow-up examination at the clinic. Day 6-7: departure. Many patients choose to extend their stay to explore Beijing's world-famous attractions such as the Great Wall, the Forbidden City, and the Temple of Heaven - all easily accessible from our clinic.
Will I be able to communicate with the medical team in English
Yes, fluently. Our international patient department includes dedicated English-speaking coordinators and several surgeons who communicate comfortably in English throughout the entire process. We also have team members proficient in Korean, Japanese, Arabic, Russian, and other languages. All consent forms, medical instructions, and aftercare guides are provided in English.
Can I have a video consultation before booking my flight
Absolutely. We offer complimentary remote consultations via WhatsApp, WeChat, Zoom, or email. Simply send clear photographs of your scalp from the front, top, sides, and back. A qualified surgeon will evaluate your case, estimate the graft count needed, recommend the most appropriate technique, and provide a personalized treatment plan with transparent pricing. This helps you make a fully informed decision before committing to travel.

Complimentary airport pickup is included from both Beijing Capital International Airport (PEK) and Daxing International Airport (PKX). We also assist with hotel reservations at partner properties located near the clinic, typically at rates 15-20% below publicly available prices. How is post-operative follow-up handled once I return home
Follow-up is conducted remotely through scheduled video calls and photo submissions at 1 week, 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, and 12 months post-surgery. Your operating surgeon personally reviews your progress at each checkpoint. We remain accessible via WhatsApp and email for any questions or concerns throughout your recovery journey. If you ever need an in-person follow-up, we can coordinate with a trusted partner clinic in your country.
Is Beijing safe for international travelers
Beijing is consistently ranked as one of the safest major cities in the world. Violent crime is extremely rare, and the city has an extensive public transportation network including a modern metro system. Our clinic is located in a central, well-connected area. We provide comprehensive travel guidance including safe areas to stay, reliable taxi apps (DiDi), and emergency contact numbers. Many of our patients explore the city independently during their recovery period without any issues.
What if I need to reschedule or cancel my appointment
We understand that travel plans can change. If you need to reschedule, please notify us at least 14 days before your planned surgery date and we will accommodate you at the next available slot with no additional charges. Cancellations within 7 days of the procedure may incur a small administrative fee. Deposits are fully transferable to a new date. We always work with our patients to find a solution that works for both parties.
