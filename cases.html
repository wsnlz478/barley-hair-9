Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions our patients ask most often

Are the before-and-after photos in your gallery from real patients
Every single image in our gallery belongs to an actual Barley patient. We never use stock photography, computer-generated renders, or retouched results. Each case is documented with the patient's informed consent for photo sharing. You can see the genuine quality and consistency of our work across hundreds of documented cases.
Can I find cases similar to my own hair loss pattern
Yes. During your consultation, send us clear photos of your scalp and we'll pull case files from our database that match your Norwood classification, hair texture, and aesthetic goals. Whether you're dealing with a receding hairline, crown thinning, or advanced baldness, we'll show you results from patients who started from a very similar position.
What does hair growth look like in the first 3 months
After the initial healing phase, the transplanted hairs typically shed between weeks two and six. This is completely normal and is called "shock loss." Don't worry — the follicle remains alive beneath the skin. For most patients, the first signs of new growth appear around month three, when fine, thin hairs begin to emerge from the scalp.
When do the transplanted hairs reach their full thickness
The hair shafts progressively thicken and darken between months six and twelve. By month six, you'll see noticeable improvement in coverage. At nine months, density becomes quite substantial. The final result — with full shaft maturation and maximum density — is typically visible between twelve and eighteen months. Patience during this process is key.
Will the transplanted hair look different from my natural hair
Not at all. The transplanted follicles are your own hair, taken from the permanent donor zone at the back of your head. They retain their original color, texture, and growth characteristics. Because our 360-degree placement system positions each graft at the exact natural angle, the results are visually indistinguishable from your original hair.
How do you ensure the hairline looks natural and not artificial
Our surgeons combine AI-assisted facial proportion mapping with years of artistic experience to design a hairline that suits your age, face shape, and ethnic background. We place single-hair grafts along the frontal edge for a soft, slightly irregular appearance, then gradually increase to two- and three-hair grafts further back. This mimics exactly how natural hairlines grow.
What is the "shock loss" period and should I be concerned
Shock loss refers to the temporary shedding of transplanted hairs — and occasionally some existing hairs near the treatment area. It typically occurs two to six weeks after surgery. This is a normal physiological response as the follicles enter a brief resting phase before producing new growth. The follicles themselves remain healthy beneath the skin and will produce new hair within a few months.
Do all of the transplanted grafts survive and grow
With our microneedle technique and cold-chain graft storage, we consistently achieve survival rates above 95 percent. That means nearly every follicle we transplant will produce hair. Minor variation is normal and expected, but our survival rates rank among the highest documented in the industry.
Will I need a second transplant session for full results
The majority of patients achieve their desired look in a single session. However, those with extensive hair loss (Norwood 5-7) or who want maximum density may opt for a second procedure ten to twelve months later to add more grafts. Your surgeon will give you an honest assessment of what one session can realistically accomplish during your initial consultation.
Can other people tell I've had a hair transplant
In the first week or two, there may be minor redness visible if you keep your hair very short. After that, the tiny extraction sites fade and become virtually undetectable. Because the hair grows naturally from your own follicles at proper angles, the end result looks completely natural. Most patients report that friends and colleagues notice they "look different" or "look younger" without being able to pinpoint why.
How long do the transplanted results last
The follicles taken from your permanent donor zone are genetically resistant to DHT, the hormone responsible for pattern hair loss. This means the transplanted hair will continue growing for the rest of your life. That said, your existing native hair may continue to thin over time, which is why we sometimes recommend medication to protect non-transplanted areas.
Do you have results from patients who traveled from abroad
Yes. We have extensive case documentation from international patients across North America, Europe, Australia, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East. We can share results from patients whose travel situation mirrors yours, including the timeline they followed for their visit, how they managed recovery once back home, and their remote follow-up experience.
What affects the final density I can achieve
Three factors determine your achievable density: the quantity and quality of your donor hair, the surface area that needs coverage, and your individual hair shaft thickness. Patients with coarser, thicker hair achieve more visible coverage per graft than those with fine, thin hair. Your surgeon evaluates all of these variables to set realistic density expectations before any procedure.
Can women achieve similar results to men in your gallery
Yes. While female hair loss patterns differ from male patterns, our female patients achieve equally transformative results. Women typically present with diffuse thinning rather than complete baldness, and our precision microneedle technique is particularly well-suited for placing grafts between existing hairs. Browse our Women's Hair Restoration category to see authentic before-and-after transformations.
How do I track my own progress after the procedure
Every patient receives a structured photo documentation guide with exact angles and lighting conditions to use. Take monthly photos from the same positions and compare them side by side. You'll also submit these photos to your surgeon during scheduled follow-ups. Many patients create their own progress albums and are genuinely amazed at the month-by-month improvement.
What if my results don't match the photos I see in the gallery
Every patient is different, and individual results vary based on hair characteristics, donor supply, and the extent of hair loss. During your consultation, your surgeon will show you cases that closely match your situation and set realistic expectations. If your outcome falls below our guaranteed survival rate, we offer complimentary corrective procedures to address the issue.
