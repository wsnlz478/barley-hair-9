What Is FUE Hair Transplant

The most advanced and widely requested hair restoration technique worldwide

Follicular Unit Extraction (FUE) is widely regarded as the gold standard in hair restoration. Unlike the older FUT (strip) method, which requires a surgical incision and leaves a linear scar, FUE harvests individual hair follicles one at a time from the donor area — typically the back and sides of your head. These healthy follicles are then carefully placed into thinning or balding regions, where they grow naturally and permanently.

Because FUE works at the individual follicle level, there are no scalpels, no stitches, and virtually no visible scarring. Recovery is fast, and most patients return to work within a few days. At Barley, we've taken FUE a step further with our proprietary 0.6mm microneedle system, which minimizes tissue damage and delivers an industry-leading 98% graft survival rate. Simply put, nearly every transplanted follicle thrives.

How FUE Works: Step by Step

From donor assessment to your new hairline — a proven four-step process

Step 1: Donor Area Assessment

Our specialists evaluate your donor area using high-magnification imaging to identify the strongest, most viable follicles for extraction.

Step 2: Gentle Follicle Extraction

Each follicle is carefully removed using our 0.6mm micro-punch, creating tiny extraction points that heal within days without leaving visible marks.

Step 3: Graft Preparation

Harvested follicles are sorted under microscopes and kept in a specialized nutrient solution to maintain viability before implantation.

Step 4: Strategic Implantation

Using our proprietary implant pen, each graft is positioned at the exact angle, depth, and direction to perfectly match your natural hair growth pattern.

Frequently Asked Questions About FUE

Honest answers to the questions patients ask most

What exactly is FUE and how does the extraction process work
FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction) is a minimally invasive hair restoration technique that harvests individual hair follicles directly from your donor area — typically the back and sides of your scalp. Using specialized micro-instruments, our surgeons carefully extract each follicular unit one at a time. These healthy grafts are then prepared and strategically placed into thinning or balding regions. The entire procedure is performed under local anesthesia, keeping you comfortable from start to finish.
Is the FUE procedure painful
The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, so you won't feel pain during extraction or implantation. Most patients describe the sensation as mild pressure rather than discomfort. After the anesthesia wears off, you may experience slight soreness or tightness for a day or two, which is easily managed with over-the-counter pain relief. The vast majority of our patients say the experience was far more comfortable than they expected.
How does FUE compare to FUT (strip method)
The key difference is how follicles are harvested. FUT (Follicular Unit Transplantation) involves removing a strip of scalp from the back of the head, which leaves a linear scar and requires a longer recovery. FUE extracts individual follicles one by one, leaving no linear scar and enabling a much faster recovery. FUE also offers greater flexibility in donor area usage and allows for body hair transplantation when scalp donor supply is limited. Today, over 90% of patients worldwide choose FUE over FUT.
How long does an FUE hair transplant session take
The duration depends on the number of grafts being transplanted. A typical session involving 2,000— ,000 grafts takes approximately 6— hours. Larger procedures may be split across two days to ensure optimal graft handling and surgical precision. We never rush the process — careful, meticulous extraction and placement are essential for achieving the best possible outcome.
How long is the recovery, and when can I go back to work
Recovery from FUE is remarkably fast. Most patients return to office work within 3— days. Small scabs in the donor and recipient areas typically resolve within 7— 0 days. You can resume light exercise after one week and return to full physical activity (including heavy gym workouts) after about 3— weeks. Many patients schedule their procedure on a Thursday and are back at their desk the following Monday.
Will the transplanted hair look natural
Absolutely. At Barley, our surgeons carefully match the angle, direction, and density of the transplanted follicles to your existing natural hair growth pattern. This attention to detail ensures seamless blending that is virtually indistinguishable from your original hair. Even in close-up or under bright lighting, our results look completely natural. Many patients find that even close friends and family members cannot tell they've had a procedure.
Will the transplanted hair grow permanently
Yes. Follicles harvested from the donor zone are genetically resistant to DHT (dihydrotestosterone), the hormone responsible for pattern hair loss. Once transplanted and established in their new location, they retain this resistance and continue growing for the rest of your life. The transplanted hair behaves exactly like your natural hair — it grows, can be cut and styled, and will grey naturally as you age. FUE is designed to be a one-time, permanent solution.
What is Barley's graft survival rate, and why does it matter
At Barley, we consistently achieve a 98% graft survival rate, which is well above the global industry average of 85— 0%. This means that nearly every transplanted follicle successfully takes root and grows. A higher survival rate translates directly to fuller, denser, and more natural-looking results. Our exceptional rate is achieved through our 0.6mm microneedle extraction, temperature-controlled graft storage, patented 360implant pen, and the expertise of senior surgeons with 10+ years of experience.
Can I shave my head after FUE surgery
Yes, absolutely. One of the greatest advantages of FUE over the older strip method (FUT) is that it leaves no linear scar. The tiny extraction points heal as microscopic dots that are virtually invisible, even with a completely shaved head or very short buzz cut. Many of our patients who prefer short hairstyles specifically choose FUE for this reason.
What is "shock loss" and should I be concerned
Shock loss refers to the natural shedding of transplanted hairs that typically occurs 2— weeks after the procedure. This is completely normal and expected. While the hair shafts fall out, the follicles themselves remain alive and healthy beneath your skin, simply entering a resting phase before new growth begins. New hair typically starts emerging around months 3— , so there's no cause for concern during this temporary shedding period.
How many grafts can be harvested in a single session
In a single day, our experienced surgical teams can typically extract and implant 2,500— ,500 grafts. The exact number depends on your donor area quality, the size of the treatment zone, and your individual goals. For patients requiring extensive restoration, we may recommend splitting the procedure into two sessions to ensure optimal graft survival and surgical precision. Your surgeon will determine the ideal graft count during your consultation.
What makes Barley's 0.6mm microneedle technology different
Most clinics use extraction tools ranging from 0.8mm to 1.0mm in diameter. Barley's proprietary microneedles measure just 0.6mm — nearly half the size of conventional instruments. This smaller diameter means less damage to surrounding scalp tissue, faster healing, reduced swelling, and extraction points that heal virtually invisible. Combined with our patented implant pen and specialized graft preservation protocol, we consistently achieve results that exceed what most clinics can deliver.
How should I prepare for my FUE procedure
Preparation is straightforward. We recommend avoiding blood-thinning medications (such as aspirin), alcohol, and smoking for at least one week before your procedure, as these can affect healing and graft survival. You'll receive detailed pre-procedure instructions from our team, including dietary guidelines and hair washing recommendations. We also suggest arranging for someone to accompany you on surgery day and planning for 2— days of light recovery at home afterward.
Can FUE be used if I've already had FUT (strip) surgery
Yes, FUE is an excellent option for patients who have previously undergone FUT strip surgery. It allows us to harvest follicles from areas unaffected by the previous strip scar, maximizing your remaining donor supply. FUE can also be used to transplant hair directly into existing FUT scars to help camouflage them. Our surgeons will evaluate your donor area during consultation to determine the best approach for your situation.

What happens if some grafts don't survive the procedure
While our 98% graft survival rate means the vast majority of follicles thrive, we understand that a small percentage may not survive in rare cases. Barley stands behind our results with a comprehensive follow-up program. If you experience lower-than-expected growth after the full 12— 8 month healing period, our surgeons will evaluate your case and discuss options, which may include a complimentary touch-up procedure if medically appropriate.

Can FUE extract hair from body areas other than the scalp
Yes. When scalp donor hair is insufficient, FUE allows us to harvest follicles from other areas of your body, including the beard, chest, and back. These body hair follicles can be transplanted to the scalp or used for facial hair restoration. While body hair has slightly different growth characteristics than scalp hair, our experienced surgeons know how to integrate it seamlessly for natural-looking results. This versatility makes FUE suitable even for patients with very limited scalp donor supply.
