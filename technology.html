FUE Technique

FUE Hair Transplant

Follicular Unit Extraction (FUE) is the gold standard in modern hair restoration. Individual follicular units are harvested from the donor area using precision micro-punches, then implanted with our microneedle pen for natural-looking results with virtually no visible scarring.

Minimally invasive procedure
No linear scarring
Quick recovery time
Natural hair growth

FUT Technique

FUT Hair Transplant

Follicular Unit Transplantation (FUT) involves harvesting a strip of donor tissue, which is then meticulously dissected into individual follicular units. While FUE is our primary recommendation, FUT remains a viable option for patients requiring maximum graft counts in a single session.

Higher graft yield
Cost-effective for large areas
Proven effectiveness
Expert surgical technique

Our Hair Transplant Process

Your complete 9-step journey to natural hair restoration

01

Consultation & Scalp Assessment

Your surgeon evaluates your scalp, grades your hair loss, and assesses donor area follicle density to confirm you're a good candidate for transplantation.

02

Personalized Hairline Design

Your surgeon hand-draws a customized hairline that complements your facial proportions, age, and personal preferences. We plan every implantation zone and calculate the exact graft count needed.

03

Pre-Procedure Health Screening

Standard blood work and coagulation tests to ensure there are no medical concerns. Your safety is our first priority.

04

Local Anesthesia

Targeted numbing of the donor and recipient areas. You'll stay awake and comfortable throughout, with no pain during the procedure.

05

Donor Follicle Harvesting

Individual follicular units are carefully extracted from the donor area at the back of your head using precision micro-punches that leave only tiny, virtually invisible marks.

06

Graft Preparation & Sorting

Our technicians carefully clean, sort, and store harvested follicles in a temperature-controlled nutrient solution to keep them at peak viability until implantation.

07

Precision Graft Implantation

Healthy follicles are meticulously placed one by one using our patented microneedle pen. The 360rotation ensures each graft matches the natural growth direction of surrounding hair.

08

Immediate Post-Procedure Care

The scalp is gently cleaned and fitted with a light protective dressing. We make sure you're comfortable and provide detailed washing instructions before you leave.

09

Aftercare & Follow-Up

You'll receive comprehensive written instructions on washing, daily activities, and what to expect during recovery. We schedule follow-up check-ins to monitor your progress every step of the way.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our microneedle hair transplant technology

How does the microneedle implant pen actually work during surgery
The surgeon loads a single follicular unit into the hollow needle tip of our proprietary pen. Instead of creating a slit first and then inserting the graft with forceps, the pen does both simultaneously. As the needle enters the scalp, the surgeon depresses the plunger, depositing the graft at the exact predetermined depth and angle. This one-step process eliminates the time gap between incision and placement.
Why is 360-degree rotation so important for natural results
Hair doesn't grow straight up from your scalp. Each follicle emerges at a unique angle and direction that varies across different zones of your head. The rotating barrel on our implant pen allows the surgeon to twist each graft into perfect alignment with your natural growth pattern as it's being placed. Without this rotation, hair can emerge in wrong directions, creating an obvious "pluggy" or unnatural appearance.
What exactly makes the 0.6mm needle size so significant
At 0.6mm, our smallest needle is roughly one-third thinner than traditional transplant tools. This means the wound channel is dramatically smaller, causing less disruption to surrounding tissue. Smaller wounds heal faster, bleed less, swell less, and allow us to pack grafts closer together for denser results. Think of it like the difference between a pinprick and a paper cut.
Is it really safe to wash my hair after just 24 hours
Yes, and here's why: because the incisions are only 0.6-1.0mm wide, they close and begin sealing within a few hours. By the next morning, the grafts are securely anchored and the entry points have formed a natural protective barrier. We'll teach you the gentle washing technique using lukewarm water and a mild, clinic-provided shampoo. Traditional methods with larger wounds require 5-7 days before any contact with water.
How do you achieve 20-30% higher density compared to standard FUE
Two factors drive the density advantage. First, the tiny needle size means each graft occupies less space, allowing closer spacing between adjacent follicles without restricting blood flow. Second, because we don't need to pre-make recipient slits, we can adjust spacing in real-time during implantation, optimizing distribution for maximum visual coverage. The result is a fuller appearance with the same number of grafts.
Can this technique work for women experiencing diffuse thinning
Absolutely, and it's one of our most popular applications for female patients. Women's hair loss typically presents as widespread thinning rather than complete bald patches. The precision of our 0.6mm needle lets us implant individual grafts between existing thinning hairs without damaging the native follicles. The result is a seamless blend that looks completely natural, even under close inspection.
What's the difference between Barley's system and DHI (Direct Hair Implantation)
DHI is a general term for implant-pen methods. The most well-known DHI tool is the Choi Implanter Pen. While both approaches load grafts into a pen for direct placement, Barley's system adds two critical advantages: our patented 360-degree rotating mechanism that the Choi pen lacks, and a finer needle diameter starting at 0.6mm versus Choi's minimum of approximately 0.7mm. These differences translate to better angular control and faster healing.
How does Barley's microneedle approach compare to Sapphire FUE
Sapphire FUE uses blades made from sapphire crystal to cut recipient sites, then grafts are placed separately with forceps. That's a two-step process. Our microneedle system performs incision and placement in one continuous motion using the implant pen. The single-step approach reduces graft handling time by over 60%, lowers the risk of mechanical damage during transfer, and enables denser packing because the surgeon can feel the tissue resistance in real-time.
What are the 14 patents that Barley holds
Our patent portfolio spans the entire transplant workflow: the core implant pen design with rotation mechanism, depth-control systems for consistent placement, follicle protection technology to prevent graft damage during handling, atraumatic extraction devices, specialized beard harvesting tools, cold-chain storage protocols for maximum viability, precision forceps for delicate manipulation, and density-optimization spacing algorithms. These patents represent nearly two decades of continuous innovation.
Can the implant pen technique be used for eyebrow restoration
Yes, and eyebrow restoration actually benefits enormously from this technology. Eyebrow hairs grow at very specific angles that differ from scalp hair. The 360-degree rotation allows us to mimic the natural feather-like direction of each brow hair with precision that forceps-based methods simply can't match. We use even finer needle configurations specifically calibrated for the delicate eyebrow area.
How is the donor area harvested during an FUE procedure
Our surgical team uses motorized extraction punches ranging from 0.8mm to 1.0mm in diameter. Each punch gently scores around an individual follicular unit in the donor zone at the back of your head. The follicle is then carefully lifted out and immediately transferred to a temperature-controlled holding solution. Our extraction and implantation teams work in parallel to minimize the time each graft spends outside your body.
When would you recommend FUT over FUE
We primarily use our microneedle FUE system. FUT (strip method) is reserved for specific situations: patients who need an exceptionally large number of grafts in a single session and have limited donor density, or cases where the patient's hair characteristics make strip harvesting more practical. FUT does leave a linear scar, so we discuss this trade-off thoroughly during your assessment.
What does a no-shave hair transplant involve
In a standard procedure, the donor area is trimmed short for visibility. With our no-shave option, the surgical team extracts individual follicles from between your existing longer hairs, and the recipient area is never shaved at all. This means nobody can tell you've had a procedure. The trade-off is that the surgery takes longer because the team must work around your existing hair. Results are identical to a standard approach.
What temperature are grafts stored at during the procedure
Every extracted follicle is placed in a specialized nutrient-rich preservation solution maintained between 4-8 degrees Celsius (39-46 degrees Fahrenheit). This chilled environment dramatically slows cellular metabolism, extending the viable window for each graft. Our proprietary storage system, covered under our patents, is one key reason why our survival rates consistently exceed 98% even during longer procedures.
Does Barley incorporate AI or automated systems in the procedure
We use AI-powered software for pre-operative hairline design. The system analyzes your facial proportions, age, ethnic characteristics, and personal preferences to recommend the most natural hairline position and shape. However, the actual extraction and implantation are performed entirely by experienced surgeons. We believe surgical artistry and human judgment remain irreplaceable for achieving truly natural results.
How many grafts can be transplanted in a single session
A typical session covers 2,000-3,500 grafts and takes 6-8 hours. For patients needing more extensive coverage, we can transplant up to 4,000+ grafts by extending the procedure across two consecutive days. The limiting factor isn't the technology but rather your available donor supply and the safe duration you can comfortably remain under local anesthesia. Your surgeon will recommend the optimal graft count during your consultation.
