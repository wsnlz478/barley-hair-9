Common Questions About Our Procedures

Discover answers to frequently asked questions by our patients

How do I know which hair transplant procedure is best for me
The best procedure for you depends on your specific hair loss pattern, donor hair availability, and desired outcomes. During your free consultation at Barley, our expert surgeons will evaluate your scalp, discuss your goals, and recommend the most suitable treatment— —ther that's a hairline transplant, hair thickening, or another service.
What exactly is involved in a hairline transplant at Barley
Our hairline transplant involves harvesting individual follicles from your donor area using our patented microneedle technology, then artistically placing them along a custom-designed hairline. The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, typically takes 4-8 hours depending on graft count, and results in a natural-looking frame for your face that lasts a lifetime.
Can hair thickening be done without shaving my head
Yes! Our no-shave hair thickening option is particularly popular among international patients. We can selectively extract and implant follicles while keeping your existing hair at its current length. This allows for a more discreet procedure and faster return to your normal routine without obvious signs of having had surgery.
How many grafts do I need for crown baldness restoration
The number of grafts needed for crown restoration varies based on the extent of your baldness and desired density. Typically, crown procedures require 1,500-3,000 grafts. Our microneedle technology allows for higher density per session, so you may need fewer grafts to achieve comparable results to traditional methods.
Are eyebrow transplants painful
Eyebrow transplants are performed under local anesthesia, so you won't feel pain during the procedure. You may experience some pressure or mild tugging, but most patients find the experience very comfortable. After the procedure, mild soreness is normal and easily managed with over-the-counter pain medication.
What's the difference between a beard transplant and scalp hair transplant
While the basic principle of follicle extraction and implantation is the same, beard transplants require more precise angle and direction placement to match natural facial hair growth patterns. Our surgeons have specialized expertise in artistic facial hair design to ensure your beard or mustache looks completely natural and matches your desired style.
How long after a hair transplant can I return to work
Most patients can return to desk work within 3-5 days after their procedure. For physical labor or strenuous activities, we recommend waiting 10-14 days. Our microneedle technology minimizes trauma and speeds up healing, so you'll recover faster than with traditional FUE methods. We'll provide specific guidance based on your procedure type.
Can I combine multiple procedures in one session
Absolutely! Many patients combine hairline restoration with hair thickening, or add a beard transplant to their scalp procedure. Combining treatments in one session can be more efficient and cost-effective. Your surgeon will advise on safe graft limits per session and whether staging your treatments would yield better results.
Will my transplanted hair look natural after it grows in
Yes! Our 360rotating microneedle implant pen allows surgeons to place each follicle at the exact angle and direction of your natural hair growth. Combined with our surgeons' artistic eye for hairline design, the results are completely undetectable— —n barbers and hairstylists typically can't tell the difference.
What is PRP treatment and how does it help with hair loss
PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) treatment involves drawing a small amount of your blood, processing it to concentrate the platelets, and injecting it into your scalp. The growth factors in PRP stimulate dormant hair follicles, improve hair thickness, and can slow down hair loss. It's often recommended as a complementary treatment alongside hair transplants.
Do you offer body hair transplants for chest or pubic areas
Yes, we offer body hair transplantation for various areas including the chest, eyebrows, pubic region, and other body parts requiring enhancement. Body hair transplants use the same careful extraction and placement techniques as scalp procedures, with attention paid to matching the unique growth patterns of each body region.
What happens if my transplanted hair doesn't grow properly
With our advanced microneedle technology and experienced surgical teams, graft survival rates exceed 95%. In the rare event that some grafts don't take, we offer complimentary touch-up sessions within the first year to address any areas of concern. Your surgeon will discuss this during your consultation.
Can women get the same hair transplant procedures as men
Yes! Women can benefit from all our hair restoration services including hairline lowering, hair thickening, and eyebrow transplants. Female pattern hair loss often presents differently than male baldness, and our surgeons have specialized techniques for women. We treat many international female patients seeking hair restoration in China.
How much does each service cost for international patients
Pricing varies based on the procedure type, number of grafts needed, and technique used. Generally, hairline transplants start at approximately 2,000-3,000 USD, while full baldness restoration can range from 3,000-6,000 USD. Our prices are 50-70% lower than Western countries for the same or better quality. You'll receive a detailed quote during your free consultation.
Is hair loss treatment effective for all types of baldness
Non-surgical hair loss treatments are most effective for early-stage hair loss, diffuse thinning, and conditions like telogen effluvium. For advanced pattern baldness, a transplant is usually the most effective solution. During your consultation, we'll determine whether medical therapy, a transplant, or a combination approach is best for your specific situation.
What should I avoid before my hair transplant procedure
Before your procedure, avoid blood-thinning medications (like aspirin), alcohol, smoking, and caffeine for at least 1-2 weeks. These can affect bleeding, healing, and graft survival. We'll provide a complete pre-procedure checklist during your consultation. For international patients, we recommend arriving in China at least 2 days before your scheduled procedure.
Can I see results immediately after my hair transplant
Immediately after the procedure, you'll see the transplanted grafts in place, but the full results take time. Transplanted hair typically sheds within 2-4 weeks, then begins regrowing at 3-4 months. Final results are visible at 9-12 months as the hair thickens and matures. Patience is essential for hair transplant success!
