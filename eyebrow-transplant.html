Understanding Eyebrow Transplantation

A permanent solution for sparse, thin, or missing eyebrows

An eyebrow transplant is a specialized cosmetic procedure that restores sparse, thin, or missing eyebrow hair by relocating healthy hair follicles from a donor area — typically the back of the scalp — to the brow region. The result is permanent, natural-looking eyebrows that grow just like your original brow hair and can be trimmed and styled as desired.

Unlike microblading or cosmetic tattooing — which merely create the illusion of fuller brows using pigment on the skin's surface — an eyebrow transplant uses your own living hair follicles. The outcome is three-dimensional, authentic, and truly permanent. Because the transplanted hair is genuinely yours, it responds naturally to humidity, temperature, and your skin's natural oils, behaving exactly as real eyebrow hair should.

Successful eyebrow transplantation requires exceptional surgical artistry and precision. Eyebrow hairs grow at highly specific angles and directions that differ dramatically from scalp hair. Our surgeons use specialized techniques for brow restoration, placing each individual graft at the precise 10— 5 degree angle that perfectly mimics natural eyebrow growth patterns. The result is brows that look completely authentic — never like transplanted scalp hair.

Who Can Benefit from an Eyebrow Transplant

Common reasons patients seek eyebrow restoration

Over-Plucked Eyebrows

Years of repeated tweezing, waxing, or threading can permanently damage eyebrow follicles beyond repair. Our transplant restores the full, natural shape you desire — even when natural regrowth is no longer possible.

Scar-Related Brow Loss

Accidents, burns, facial surgery, or trauma that have left scars in your brow area can be effectively treated. Our surgeons transplant healthy follicles directly into scar tissue to rebuild a seamless, natural-looking eyebrow.

Naturally Sparse or Thin Brows

Some people are simply born with thin, patchy, or barely-visible eyebrows. Our procedure can create the fuller, more defined brows you've always wanted — with results that last a lifetime.

Medical Hair Loss Conditions

Conditions like alopecia areata, thyroid disorders, hormonal imbalances, or chemotherapy can cause partial or complete eyebrow loss. Once your underlying condition is stable, we can permanently restore your eyebrows.

Our Eyebrow Transplant Technique

Where surgical precision meets artistic vision

Where Artistry Meets Surgical Precision

Step 1 — Custom Brow Design: Before surgery begins, our surgeon draws your new eyebrow shape directly on your face using a surgical marker. We work closely with you to achieve the ideal shape, thickness, and arch that complements your unique facial features. Your input is essential — we refine the design together until you are completely satisfied with the planned result.

Step 2 — Single-Hair Graft Selection: Eyebrow restoration exclusively uses single-hair grafts for the most natural appearance. Our technicians carefully select the finest, most delicate follicles from your donor area, as these produce the softest, most natural-looking eyebrow hairs.

Step 3 — Ultra-Precise Placement: Using our finest microneedle instruments, each graft is placed at the exact angle and direction of natural eyebrow growth. The front portion of the brow grows upward and outward, the middle section lies flatter, and the tail angles downward. This multi-directional approach is what creates truly authentic-looking eyebrows.

Step 4 — Strategic Density Mapping: We create a natural gradient density — fuller in the body of the brow and gradually softer toward the front and tail. This graduated look is characteristic of natural eyebrows and avoids the artificial "solid block" appearance that less experienced clinics produce.

Eyebrow Transplant: Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions our patients ask most often

What exactly is an eyebrow transplant
An eyebrow transplant is a minimally invasive surgical procedure that relocates healthy hair follicles — typically from the back of your scalp — to the eyebrow area. The transplanted hairs grow naturally in their new location, creating permanent, full eyebrows that look completely authentic. Unlike microblading or cosmetic tattooing, the results are real, three-dimensional hair that you can trim, shape, and style.
How many grafts will I need for an eyebrow transplant
A single eyebrow typically requires 150— 00 grafts, depending on the extent of hair loss and the fullness you desire. For both eyebrows, most patients need between 300 and 600 total grafts. Your surgeon will provide a precise estimate during your consultation after assessing your current brow condition and discussing your goals.
Will the transplanted eyebrow hair look natural
Yes — when performed by experienced surgeons, eyebrow transplants are virtually indistinguishable from natural brows. Our surgeons place each graft at the precise angle and direction of natural eyebrow growth, use only single-hair grafts for the most delicate appearance, and create a graduated density pattern that mimics how real eyebrows grow. The result looks completely natural from every angle.
Can I choose the shape and style of my new eyebrows
Absolutely — your input is a vital part of the design process. Before surgery, our surgeon draws the proposed eyebrow shape directly on your face using a surgical marker. You review the shape, provide feedback, and we adjust the thickness, arch, and length until you are completely happy with the planned result. Our surgeons also offer professional guidance to ensure the shape complements your face shape, eye placement, and personal style.
Is the eyebrow transplant procedure painful
The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, so you will feel no pain during surgery. Most patients describe the experience as surprisingly comfortable — many relax, listen to music, or even nap during the procedure. Once the anesthesia wears off, you may experience mild tenderness for a day or two, which is easily managed with over-the-counter pain relief.
How long does the procedure take
A bilateral eyebrow transplant (both eyebrows) typically takes 3 to 5 hours. Restoring a single eyebrow takes approximately 2 to 3 hours. The exact duration depends on the number of grafts required and the complexity of your custom design. The entire procedure is completed in a single session, and you go home the same day.
What is the recovery time after an eyebrow transplant
Recovery is relatively quick. Small scabs will form around the graft sites and naturally fall off within 5 to 7 days. Some mild swelling around the eyes is normal and typically resolves within 2 to 3 days. Most patients feel comfortable returning to work and normal social activities within a week. The donor area on the back of your scalp heals within just a few days.
When will I see my final results
The transplanted eyebrow hairs will shed within 2 to 4 weeks after surgery — this is completely normal and expected. New growth begins around month 3. By month 6, you will see roughly 70 to 80 percent of your final result. Full, mature results are visible between 9 and 12 months once all transplanted hairs have fully grown in and thickened.
Will I need to trim my transplanted eyebrows
Yes. Because the transplanted hair originates from your scalp, it will grow at a similar rate to scalp hair. You will need to trim your eyebrows occasionally — typically once every 1 to 2 weeks. However, this is a quick and simple process that takes just a few minutes with small grooming scissors. Most patients find this to be a very small trade-off for having permanent, natural-looking eyebrows.
Can an eyebrow transplant cover scars in the brow area
Yes. Eyebrow transplant is highly effective at covering scars within the brow region. Whether the scar is from an accident, previous surgery, burns, or injury, our surgeons can transplant healthy follicles directly into the scar tissue. The graft survival rate in scar tissue is slightly lower than in normal skin, so we typically place a few extra grafts to ensure excellent, full coverage.
Is eyebrow transplant suitable for men
Yes. A growing number of men seek eyebrow transplants to address thin, sparse, or uneven brows. Male eyebrow design follows different aesthetic principles — typically creating straighter, fuller brows with a lower arch that complement masculine facial features. Our surgeons are highly experienced in designing natural-looking brows for both male and female patients.
How much does an eyebrow transplant cost
Eyebrow transplant costs vary significantly by country. In the United States, UK, or Europe, the procedure typically costs between 3,000 and 8,000 USD. At Barley in China, eyebrow transplants range from 800 to 2,500 USD, depending on the number of grafts required. This represents substantial savings while maintaining the highest international quality standards. Contact us for a personalized, no-obligation quote.
How is eyebrow transplant different from microblading
Microblading uses pigment to create the illusion of eyebrow hairs on the skin's surface — it is essentially a semi-permanent cosmetic tattoo that fades over 1 to 3 years and requires regular touch-ups. An eyebrow transplant uses your own real hair follicles, creating actual three-dimensional hair that grows, can be trimmed and styled, and lasts for life. The results look far more natural than any tattoo-based technique because they literally are natural hair.
What are the risks and side effects
Eyebrow transplant is a safe, minimally invasive procedure with a strong safety record. Temporary side effects include mild swelling, tenderness, and small scabs around the graft sites — all of which resolve naturally within 1 to 2 weeks. Rare complications may include infection, poor graft survival, or results that do not meet aesthetic expectations. Choosing an experienced, board-certified surgical team significantly minimizes these risks. We discuss all potential risks transparently during your consultation.
Are the results truly permanent
Yes. The transplanted follicles are harvested from the permanent donor zone at the back of your scalp, which is genetically resistant to hair loss. These follicles will continue to grow in their new eyebrow location for the rest of your life. However, if your original eyebrow loss was caused by an ongoing medical condition (such as thyroid disease or alopecia), it is important to manage that condition to protect both the transplanted and any remaining native eyebrow hair.
How should I prepare before my procedure
In the week leading up to your procedure, avoid alcohol and smoking. Stop taking blood-thinning medications (such as aspirin or ibuprofen) 7 to 10 days before surgery unless prescribed by your doctor. Wash your hair and face thoroughly on the morning of your procedure. Wear a button-down or zip-up top so you do not need to pull clothing over your head afterwards. Our patient coordination team will send you a detailed preparation checklist before your appointment.
Can I wear makeup on my eyebrows after the transplant
Once the treated area has fully healed — approximately 2 to 3 weeks after surgery — you can safely apply makeup to your eyebrows if you wish. However, most patients find they no longer need eyebrow pencils, gels, or powders because the transplanted hair provides the fullness and definition they were previously trying to achieve with cosmetics.
