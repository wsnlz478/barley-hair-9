Frequently Asked Questions

Honest answers to the questions patients ask most

How accurate is the hair loss calculator
Our calculator provides a general assessment based on your responses. For a precise diagnosis, we recommend scheduling a free consultation where our specialists can examine your scalp using advanced diagnostic tools and provide personalized recommendations.
What are the common causes of hair loss
Hair loss can be caused by genetics (androgenetic alopecia), hormonal changes, medical conditions, medications, stress, nutritional deficiencies, and aging. The most common cause is genetic pattern baldness, which affects both men and women. Our specialists can identify the specific cause during your consultation.
When should I seek treatment for hair loss
It's best to seek treatment as early as possible. Early intervention can slow down hair loss progression and preserve more of your existing hair. If you notice significant thinning, receding hairline, or excessive shedding (more than 100 hairs per day), we recommend scheduling a consultation.
What treatment options are available
Treatment options include hair transplant surgery (FUE, FUT, microneedle), medical treatments (minoxidil, finasteride), PRP therapy, and hair care maintenance. The best approach depends on your hair loss stage, goals, and overall health. Our specialists will recommend the most suitable options during your consultation.
Is hair transplant permanent
Yes, hair transplant results are permanent. The transplanted follicles are taken from the donor area (typically the back of the head), which is genetically resistant to hair loss. Once transplanted, these follicles continue to grow naturally for the rest of your life.
How much does treatment cost at Barley
Costs vary depending on the treatment type and extent of hair loss. Our pricing is typically 50-70% more affordable than equivalent clinics in North America, Europe, or Australia. Contact us for a free personalized quote with transparent pricing and no hidden fees.
