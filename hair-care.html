Post-Transplant Hair Care: What You Need to Know

The first two weeks after surgery are critical. Freshly implanted grafts are fragile and need time to establish blood supply and anchor into your scalp. During this period, following your surgeon's washing instructions exactly is the single most important thing you can do. Use only lukewarm water and the recommended gentle shampoo. Never scratch, rub, or pick at scabs — let them fall off naturally. Sleep with your head elevated to reduce swelling, and avoid exercise, swimming, and direct sun exposure until your doctor gives you the green light.

Once the initial healing phase is over, keeping your scalp healthy is key to maintaining both your transplanted and existing hair. Stick to sulfate-free, mild hair care products that don't strip your scalp's natural oils. Many patients find that biotin supplements, a balanced diet rich in protein and iron, and occasional PRP treatments help speed up and improve the quality of new growth. Avoid harsh chemical treatments, excessive heat styling, and tight hairstyles that put stress on your follicles. Every patient at Barley Hospital receives a personalized aftercare plan and ongoing access to our clinical team for as long as they need.

Essential Aftercare Practices

How to Wash Properly

Use lukewarm water and a gentle, sulfate-free shampoo. For the first week, pour water over your scalp with a cup — never use a direct shower stream. Pat dry with a soft towel; never rub. After two weeks, you can wash normally using light fingertip motions, keeping your nails away from the scalp.

Protecting Your Scalp from the Sun

Your scalp is highly sensitive to sun damage for at least 4-6 weeks after surgery. When outdoors, wear a loose-fitting hat or apply mineral-based SPF 30+ sunscreen once healing allows. Prolonged sun exposure can cause discoloration and slow down graft recovery.

Nutrition for Hair Growth

A diet rich in protein, iron, zinc, biotin, and vitamins A, C, D, and E gives your body the building blocks it needs for strong hair growth. Eat eggs, nuts, leafy greens, fish, and lean meats regularly. Poor nutrition can slow recovery and weaken new hair.

Regular Follow-Up Appointments

Scheduled check-ups at 1, 3, 6, and 12 months let our doctors track your graft growth, catch any issues early, and adjust your care routine. These appointments are included in your treatment package and make sure you're on track for the best possible results.

Common Questions About Hair Care

Expert answers for post-transplant and daily hair maintenance

When can I wash my hair after a transplant
You can begin gentle washing 48-72 hours after surgery using lukewarm water and a mild, sulfate-free shampoo. During the first week, pour water gently over your scalp rather than using direct shower spray. After 10-14 days, you can gradually return to your normal washing routine using gentle circular motions with your fingertips.
What shampoo should I use after a hair transplant
We recommend a gentle, sulfate-free, paraben-free shampoo with a balanced pH. Avoid products containing harsh detergents, artificial fragrances, or alcohol. Our clinic provides a specialized post-transplant shampoo containing panthenol and aloe vera to soothe the scalp and support graft healing during the critical first weeks.
How should I sleep after a hair transplant
For the first week, sleep on your back with your head elevated at approximately 45 degrees using extra pillows or a wedge cushion. This positioning reduces swelling and prevents accidental friction against the transplanted area. Avoid sleeping on your side or stomach during the initial healing phase.
Can I wear a hat after a hair transplant
Yes, but timing matters. Avoid any headwear for the first 3-5 days. After that, you may wear a loose-fitting, clean hat (like a bucket hat or surgeon's cap) that doesn't touch the grafts. Avoid tight caps, beanies, or helmets for at least 2-3 weeks to prevent pressure on the healing scalp.
How long does the scalp take to heal after transplant
Surface healing occurs within 7-14 days, with scabs typically falling off naturally by day 10-14. Full internal healing takes 3-6 months. The transplanted follicles enter a resting phase (shock loss) around weeks 2-4, then begin producing new hair from month 3-4 onwards. Complete results are visible at 12-18 months.
What products should I avoid after a transplant
Avoid any products containing sulfates, parabens, alcohol, or strong fragrances for at least 3 months post-surgery. Stay away from styling gels, hair sprays, mousse, and dry shampoos during the first month. Chemical treatments like dyes, perms, and bleaching should be avoided for a minimum of 6 months.
Can I exercise after a hair transplant
Light walking is fine immediately after surgery. Avoid strenuous exercise, heavy lifting, and activities that cause sweating for at least 2 weeks. Swimming should be avoided for 4 weeks. You can gradually resume your full exercise routine after 3-4 weeks, once grafts are securely established.
How do I reduce swelling after a hair transplant
Sleep with your head elevated for the first week, apply cold compresses to your forehead (not directly on grafts), stay well-hydrated, and avoid salty foods. Swelling typically peaks around day 3-4 and subsides within 7-10 days. Our doctors may also prescribe anti-inflammatory medication if needed.
Is it normal to lose transplanted hair after surgery
Yes, this is completely normal and expected. Around weeks 2-4, the transplanted hairs shed — a phenomenon called 'shock loss.' The follicles remain alive beneath the scalp and begin producing new, permanent hair from month 3-4. This shedding phase is a standard part of the growth cycle and nothing to worry about.
How often should I wash my transplanted hair
During the first week, wash gently once daily as instructed by your surgeon. After 2 weeks, you can wash every 1-2 days depending on your scalp type. Over-washing can strip natural oils, while under-washing may lead to buildup. We provide a personalized washing schedule based on your individual needs.
Can I use hair dryer after a transplant
Avoid using a hair dryer for the first 2 weeks. After that, you may use a cool or low-heat setting held at least 15cm from your scalp. High heat can damage newly healing follicles and the sensitive scalp. Air drying with a soft towel is the safest option during the initial months.
What vitamins help hair growth after transplant
Biotin (vitamin B7), zinc, iron, vitamin D, vitamin E, and omega-3 fatty acids all support healthy hair growth. A multivitamin supplement can be beneficial, but we recommend getting nutrients primarily from a balanced diet rich in protein, leafy greens, nuts, and fatty fish.
When can I color my hair after a transplant
We advise waiting at least 6 months before applying any chemical hair coloring, bleaching, or perming treatments. These chemicals can irritate the scalp and potentially damage newly established grafts. When you do color your hair, choose ammonia-free, gentle formulas and inform your colorist about your transplant.
How do I protect my scalp from the sun after transplant
For the first 4-6 weeks, wear a loose-fitting hat when outdoors. After healing, apply a mineral-based SPF 30+ sunscreen to your scalp if exposed to direct sunlight. UV radiation can cause hyperpigmentation and may compromise graft health during early recovery. Continue sun protection year-round.
Can I use minoxidil after a hair transplant
Many surgeons recommend starting minoxidil 2-4 weeks post-transplant to support graft growth and protect existing hair. However, timing varies based on individual healing. Always consult your surgeon before starting any topical medication. Minoxidil can accelerate the growth of transplanted follicles and reduce shock loss duration.
What is the best diet for hair care after transplant
Focus on a protein-rich diet with plenty of vegetables, fruits, nuts, and lean meats. Include foods high in iron (spinach, red meat), zinc (pumpkin seeds, oysters), and omega-3s (salmon, walnuts). Stay hydrated and limit processed foods, excessive sugar, and alcohol, which can slow healing and affect hair quality.
How can international patients manage hair care after returning home
We provide a comprehensive aftercare package including written instructions, product recommendations available internationally, and scheduled video follow-ups at 1, 3, 6, and 12 months. Our English-speaking team remains available via WhatsApp and email for any questions. Many recommended products can be purchased locally or shipped to your country.
